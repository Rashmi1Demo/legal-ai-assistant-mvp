Contract Termination Review Checklist

1. Confirm the governing agreement and identify the termination clause.
2. Check the required notice period.
3. Check whether termination is for convenience, cause, or breach.
4. Review cure periods and notice requirements.
5. Identify any surviving obligations, confidentiality, payment, or return-of-property requirements.
6. Escalate the matter to the responsible lawyer before communicating a final legal position to the client.
